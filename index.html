Around Seattle

W A S D or arrow keys to move. E to climb or jump; Q to descend when flying.

1–5 change travel mode. V changes camera. Drag the view to orbit; scroll to zoom. M opens the map. Choose a destination in Places or click the map to travel.

5 joins a Blue Angels diamond. The jets fly forward automatically: W/S adjusts speed, A/D banks and turns, E/Q climbs and descends. B or Smoke toggles the trails. Touch uses the stick and altitude arrows; gamepads use the existing flight controls. Places views and dialogs pause formation travel.

Press F or tap a nearby discovery prompt to investigate. Another clue cycles nearby secrets. Look frames the surprise; moving or changing camera returns to travel.
